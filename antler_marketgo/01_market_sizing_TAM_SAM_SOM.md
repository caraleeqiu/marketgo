# Market Sizing: marketgo (TAM, SAM, SOM)

This document outlines the estimated market size for marketgo, a marketing tool targeting independent developers. 

**Disclaimer:** This analysis is based on publicly available data and estimations from my internal knowledge base, as direct web search capabilities are currently unavailable. All assumptions are clearly stated.

---

### 1. Hypothesis (核心假设)

1.  **Primary Target Audience:** Our core customers are "Independent Developers" (Indie Hackers, Solopreneurs, Bootstrappers) who build and launch their own digital products (SaaS, mobile apps, info products).
2.  **Core Problem:** These developers often excel at building but struggle with or lack time for consistent marketing and building in public.
3.  **Willingness to Pay:** They are willing to pay for tools that automate or simplify marketing tasks, save time, and directly contribute to user acquisition and revenue. We estimate a conservative monthly spend for such a tool.

---

### 2. Calculation Logic (测算逻辑)

*   **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** The total global market for software development tools and services. A simpler, more relevant TAM for us is the total number of indie developers globally multiplied by their potential annual spend on a marketing tool like ours.
    *   `TAM = (Total Global Indie Developers) x (Annual Average Revenue Per User - ARPU)`
*   **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** The segment of the market that our product can realistically serve. This will be indie developers who are actively building public-facing products (not just consultants or freelancers) and are likely to use marketing automation tools.
    *   `SAM = TAM x (% of Indies building products) x (% aware of marketing automation tools)`
*   **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** The portion of the SAM we can realistically capture in the first 2-3 years, considering our marketing, sales, and product capabilities.
    *   `SOM = SAM x (Realistic Market Share %)`

---

### 3. Data & Sources (数据与信源 - *Estimations*)

*   **Total Global Developers:** Estimated at **~28 million** (based on general industry reports like Evans Data Corporation, Statista, SlashData from recent years).
*   **Percentage of Independent Developers:** This is a difficult metric. We will estimate that **10%** of all developers are independent, solopreneurs, or very small teams (1-3 people) working on their own projects.
    *   *Source: Estimation based on trends seen on platforms like Indie Hackers, GitHub, and Twitter/X.*
    *   **Total Global Indie Developers:** 28,000,000 * 10% = **2,800,000**
*   **Annual ARPU:** We assume a subscription price of **$30/month**.
    *   **Annual ARPU:** $30 * 12 = **$360**
*   **% of Indies Building Public Products:** We estimate **50%** of indie developers are building products to sell, while the other 50% might be freelancers, consultants, or building internal tools.
*   **% Aware of Marketing Automation:** We estimate a high awareness of **80%** in this tech-savvy group.
*   **Realistic Market Share:** We aim to capture **1%** of our SAM in the first 2-3 years.

---

### 4. The Calculation (测算结果)

#### **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**
*   `2,800,000 (Indie Developers) x $360 (Annual ARPU)`
*   **TAM ≈ $1.008 Billion**

#### **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**
*   `$1,008,000,000 (TAM) x 50% (Building Products) x 80% (Awareness)`
*   **SAM ≈ $403 Million**

#### **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**
*   `$403,000,000 (SAM) x 1% (Market Share)`
*   **SOM ≈ $4.03 Million**

---

### Conclusion

The potential market for marketgo is substantial. Our initial obtainable market (SOM) in the first few years is estimated to be around **$4 million annually**. This represents a strong and viable starting point for a bootstrapped or early-stage venture, with a clear path to expand within the much larger Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) of over $400 million.
