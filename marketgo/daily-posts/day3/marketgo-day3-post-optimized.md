---

**Tweet 5 (Feature 1: Diagnosis):**
📊 STEP 1: Marketing Diagnosis (5 minutes)

You answer a few questions:
• What does your product do?
• Who is it for?
• What problem does it solve?

marketgo generates a one-page marketing plan.

No fluff. Just your strategy on one page.

---

**Tweet 6 (Feature 2: Audience + References):**
🎯 STEP 2: Find Your Audience (10 minutes)

marketgo tells you:
• Who your ideal user is (not "everyone")
• Which successful accounts to study
• What content style works in your niche

You get 5-10 reference accounts to learn from.

---

**Tweet 7 (Feature 3: 30-Day Schedule):**
📅 STEP 3: Your 30-Day Plan (auto-generated)

marketgo creates your daily posting schedule:

Day 1: X + Indie Hackers - "Announce your project"
Day 2: X - "Share why you built it"
Day 3: X - "First feature preview"
...
Day 25: Product Hunt Launch 🚀

Every single day planned.

---

**Tweet 8 (Feature 3 Details):**
For each day, you get:

✅ Which platform to post on
✅ What to post (topic + angle)
✅ What you need to prepare

Example:
"Day 12: Post on X about user feedback. You'll need: 1 screenshot + user quote"

No more guessing.

---

**Tweet 9 (Feature 4: Daily Execution):**
🔔 STEP 4: Daily Reminders + Assets

Every morning, marketgo sends you:

1. Reminder: "Day 12: Time to post on X"
2. Draft text (you can edit)
3. Visual assets (image or video)

You just review, tweak if needed, and publish.

---

**Tweet 10 (Assets Details):**
About those assets:

marketgo can generate:
• Images for your posts
• Videos for demos
• Graphics for data

It asks you for what it needs (screenshots, brand colors, etc.) then generates everything.

---

**Tweet 11 (The Result):**
Here's what this means for you:

❌ No more "I don't know what to post"
❌ No more spending 2 hours writing one post
❌ No more forgetting to post

✅ 30 days planned in 30 minutes
✅ Daily reminders keep you consistent
✅ Assets ready to go

**Tweet 12 (Personal Story):**
