# marketgo - Day 3 Post: Feature Preview

## 📱 TWITTER/X POST (Thread Format)

**Tweet 1 (Hook):**
Most indie hackers fail not because their product sucks.

They fail because they don't know how to market it.

Here's what marketgo does 🧵

**Tweet 2 (The Problem):**
Every day, you ask yourself:

"What should I post today?"
"Where should I post it?"
"What should I write?"
"Do I need an image?"

It's exhausting. So you just... don't post.

**Tweet 3 (The Pattern):**
I've talked to 50+ indie hackers who failed to launch.

They all said the same thing:

"I spent 6 months building, but I had no idea how to market it."

Marketing shouldn't be the hard part.

**Tweet 4 (The Solution Intro):**
That's why marketgo does 4 things:

1️⃣ Diagnose your marketing
2️⃣ Find your audience
3️⃣ Plan your 30 days
4️⃣ Remind you daily

Let me break it down:

**Tweet 5 (Feature 1):**
📊 FEATURE 1: Marketing Diagnosis

You answer a few questions about your product.

marketgo gives you a one-page marketing plan:
• Who you're building for
• Where they hang out
• What message resonates
• Your 30-day goal

One page. No fluff.

**Tweet 6 (Feature 2):**
🎯 FEATURE 2: Target Audience + Reference Accounts

marketgo tells you:
• Who your ideal user is (not "everyone")
• Which accounts to study and learn from
• What content style works in your niche

Stop guessing. Start learning from people who already succeeded.

**Tweet 7 (Feature 3):**
📅 FEATURE 3: 30-Day Social Media Schedule

marketgo generates your daily posting plan:

Day 1: Post on X + Indie Hackers - "Announce your project"
Day 2: Post on X - "Share why you built it"
Day 3: Post on X - "Show first feature"

Every day. Every platform. Every post type.

**Tweet 8 (Feature 3 continued):**
And for each day, it tells you:

✅ Which platform to post on
✅ What to post (topic + angle)
✅ What you need to prepare (screenshot? video? story?)

No more "I don't know what to post today."

**Tweet 9 (Feature 4):**
🔔 FEATURE 4: Daily Reminder + Draft + Assets

Every morning, marketgo reminds you:

"Today is Day 12. Time to post on X."

Then it gives you:
• A draft of the post (you can edit)
• Visual assets (image/video)

You just review, tweak, and publish.

**Tweet 10 (The Result):**
Here's what this means:

❌ No more "I don't know what to post"
❌ No more starting from scratch every day
❌ No more forgetting to post

✅ Just execute the plan
✅ Build your audience
✅ Launch with momentum

**Tweet 11 (Social Proof):**
I'm building this because I needed it.

My first two launches failed because I had no marketing plan.

This time, I'm following my own system.

And so far? 500+ followers, real engagement, actual interest.

**Tweet 12 (Tomorrow Teaser):**
Tomorrow I'll show you what it actually looks like.

Dashboard screenshots. Real examples. The whole UI.

If you're an indie hacker who's great at building but terrible at marketing...

Follow along. This is for you.

Day 3/30 ✅
